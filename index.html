DataStrap

Your Fitbit and Google Health data, scored on your own device.

Open DataStrap

What it does

DataStrap is a personal dashboard for people who wear a Fitbit. After you sign in with Google, it downloads your activity, sleep and heart-rate data from the Google Health API and works out:

  • Recovery: a daily 0–100 score from your heart rate variability, resting heart rate, breathing rate and skin temperature, compared with your own baseline.
  • Strain: a 0–21 score for how hard you worked, from your heart rate.
  • Sleep: stages, efficiency and an estimated sleep score.
  • Workouts: heart-rate zones, peak heart rate and heart-rate recovery for each session.

It runs as a web app in your browser (it can be added to your phone's home screen) and as an Android app.

How it uses your Google data

DataStrap asks Google for read access to your activity and fitness, sleep, health metrics and measurements, profile and settings, so it can show and score them. If you choose to log a workout in DataStrap, it also asks for permission to add that workout to your Google Health data, so it appears in the Fitbit app.

Your health data is stored on your device, not on a DataStrap server, and is never sold, shared or used for advertising. DataStrap has no advertising or analytics code. You can sign out at any time, which removes DataStrap's access to your Google account. Full details are in the Privacy Policy.
